### Thresholds
- 90–100: Production Ready
- 85–89: Strong, minor revision
- 75–84: Needs structural revision
- <75: Reject and regenerate upstream

### Automatic FAIL
- climax without choice
- action without motivation
- shot with no audience delta and no rhythm justification
- spatial contradiction
- ending template without story reason
- prompt invents new story facts
