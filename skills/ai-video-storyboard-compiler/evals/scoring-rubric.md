# Storyboard Skill Rubric

Score representative outputs on five dimensions, 0–20 each:

- **Trigger and mode:** natural Chinese storyboard requests route here; script creation and final engine prompts do not.
- **Source fidelity:** plot result and exact dialogue remain unchanged.
- **Usability:** the first response produces the requested storyboard without menus, self-reports or internal jargon.
- **Shot logic:** cuts have reasons, duration is plausible, and physical action is filmable.
- **Continuity and grouping:** start/end states connect; AI clip grouping is separate from shot numbering.

Hard failures: forced multi-step ceremony for a clear request; missing/rewritten dialogue; fixed shot count; camera jargon without visible action; every shot forced into its own generation clip.
