# 4-6 Minute Complete Short Film

## Goal

Use this format when the user wants a complete short story with a character choice, not only a skit or commercial beat. Keep short-video rhythm while delivering a full emotional or dramatic arc.

## Beat Structure

- 0-10 seconds: strong hook, crisis, abnormal result, or key image.
- 10-60 seconds: character goal and current dilemma.
- 1-2 minutes: conflict expands; relationship or interests are exposed.
- 2-4 minutes: choice, misunderstanding, reversal, or cost.
- 4-5 minutes: climax; the character makes an irreversible decision.
- Final 30-60 seconds: ending payoff that explains, upgrades, or undercuts the opening hook and leaves emotional residue.

## Character Choice Rules

- The story must contain a clear goal, obstacle, cost, and final choice.
- The climax must force an irreversible decision.
- The ending must show what the character gained, lost, or understood.
- Conflict should be built from desire, secret, misunderstanding, relationship reversal, or social pressure.
- Dialogue serves conflict; it should not explain setting.
- Visual cues serve production; do not write them as prose fiction.

## Required Output

- Logline
- Character goals
- Beginning-development-turn-resolution structure
- Scene outline
- Full script
- Self-check report
