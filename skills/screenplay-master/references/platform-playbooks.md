# Platform Playbooks

Use these as creative heuristics. Verify current platform policies before release, especially for commercial, regulated, or sensitive content.

## Douyin

- Prioritize strong first 3 seconds, vertical close-ups, fast conflict, and clear emotional payoff.
- Useful for high-density hooks, comeback, family, romance, workplace, suspense, and product drama.
- Watch for low-quality sensationalism, vulgarity, dangerous behavior, and commercial claim risk.

## Kuaishou

- Prioritize grounded characters, everyday settings, practical conflict, emotional trust, and continuity.
- Useful for family, local life, small business, underdog, and community stories.
- Keep performances direct and character motives simple.

## WeChat Channels

- Prioritize social-share value, credibility, emotional resonance, and less noisy presentation.
- Useful for brand story, public-interest short film, family, workplace, education-adjacent content.
- Avoid exaggerated clickbait if trust is the goal.

## Xiaohongshu

- Prioritize lifestyle context, personal experience, aesthetic clarity, and authentic voice.
- Useful for beauty, fashion, home, travel, education, and service scenarios, but claim risk can be high.
- Commercial disclosure and substantiation matter; avoid fake user cases.

## TikTok

- Prioritize immediate visual hook, high contrast, clear subtitles, and globally legible conflict.
- Use platform content disclosure when promoting a brand, product, or service.
- For commercial content, consider music rights and commercial-use limitations.

## YouTube Shorts

- Prioritize clear premise, retention beats, subtitle readability, and a replayable ending.
- Use paid-promotion disclosure tools when there are placements, sponsorships, endorsements, or commercial relationships.
- Series can work when titles, thumbnails, and episode continuity are clear.

## Instagram Reels

- Prioritize visual polish, trend-aware pacing, emotion, and lifestyle/brand fit.
- Keep story understandable without sound; use captions and strong composition.
- Avoid overloading plot if the visual identity is the main value.

## Short-Drama Apps

- Prioritize episode cliffhangers, genre promise, stage payoff, and paid-episode conversion.
- Each episode needs a self-contained problem plus next-episode pull.
- Every 3-5 episodes should pay off a stage of the promise.

## Adaptation Checks

- Platform: where will it release?
- Orientation: vertical, horizontal, or both?
- Runtime: seconds, minutes, episodes?
- Audience: pass-by viewer, follower, subscriber, buyer, or paid-series user?
- Primary metric: watch-through, share, comment, inquiry, purchase, follow, paid conversion?
- Risk: commercial, copyright, AI, regulated topic, minors, region?
