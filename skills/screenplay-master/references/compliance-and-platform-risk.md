# Compliance and Platform Risk

This reference is a creative risk checklist, not legal advice or platform approval. Rules change; verify current laws and platform policies before public release, especially for regulated industries or China-facing micro-drama distribution.

- NRTA micro-drama draft notice for public comment, June 24, 2026: https://www.nrta.gov.cn/art/2026/6/24/art_113_73514.html
- CAC AI-generated synthetic content labeling measures notice, March 14, 2025: https://www.cac.gov.cn/2025-03/14/c_1743654685899683.htm

## Risk Levels

Low:
- Original fictional script with no real persons, regulated products, commercial claim, or sensitive topic.

Medium:
- Product placement, fictionalized social issue, workplace/family conflict, generic platform release, light suspense or revenge.

High:
- Medical, financial, education, health, beauty, minors, real people/brands/institutions, AI-generated realism, controversial public events, violence, self-harm, dangerous behavior, explicit sexuality, IP adaptation, China public micro-drama release.

Must clarify:
- Target release region and platform are legally or commercially central.
- The user asks for a regulated product or claim.
- The user asks to use real persons, brands, institutions, or IP.
- Exact duration/budget is required but absent.

## China Micro-Drama Risk

For China-facing public release, check current micro-drama rules. The June 24, 2026 NRTA material is a public-comment draft, not a final-rule statement inside this skill. It describes micro-dramas as series with single episodes under 20 minutes, clear theme/main line, continuous story, and prominent characters. It also raises content orientation, copyright, classification/management, and AI-labeling concerns.

Flag:

- Single-episode duration and distribution channel.
- Public release in mainland China.
- Classification, filing, publication, platform review, or license requirements.
- Content involving national security, ethnic history, minors, vulgarity, materialism, violence, superstition, or other sensitive areas.
- Use of AI-generated or AI-produced content that may need labeling.

AI labeling:

- If AI generates or materially alters text, image, video, voice, character likeness, subtitle, poster, cover, or marketing material, flag labeling and platform declaration needs.
- Consider both visible labels and metadata/implicit labeling where applicable.
- Do not promise that a generated label satisfies every local rule or platform review.

## Commercial and Advertising Risk

Flag when content includes:

- Paid partnership, sponsorship, affiliate commission, free/discounted product, employment/family/personal brand relationship.
- Product placement, endorsement, creator recommendation, or brand message integrated in the story.
- Claims about efficacy, results, income, health, education, finance, beauty, or safety.

Creative handling:

- Suggest clear disclosure.
- Avoid unsupported claims and fake testimonials.
- Use platform disclosure mechanisms where required or available.
- Do not hide an ad as ordinary drama if it materially promotes a product.

## Copyright and IP

- Do not copy distinctive plots, lines, character settings, or set pieces from existing films, series, novels, short dramas, or ads.
- For IP adaptation, state the authorization assumption. If authorization is absent, provide originalization directions rather than adaptation.
- Avoid using real people, brands, or institutions in negative plots unless the user has clear factual basis and accepts release risk.
- Check music, footage, images, fonts, voices, likenesses, and source material rights.

## Platform Content Risk

Check:

- Violence, gore, self-harm, crime instruction, dangerous challenge, sexual content, minors, hate, misinformation, harassment.
- For controversial issues, use restrained expression and avoid group labeling or incitement.
- For AI-generated realistic people/events, consider labeling and deception risk.

## Disclaimer

Include a concise note when risk is material: "This is a creative risk check, not legal advice or platform approval. Verify current platform policies and local law before release."
