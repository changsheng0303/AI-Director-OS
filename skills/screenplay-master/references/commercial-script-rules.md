# Commercial Script Rules

## Product Integration

- The product, brand, course, service, or IP must have a story function.
- Valid functions: solve conflict, create misunderstanding, reveal evidence, raise stakes, enable final choice, or create CTA.
- Avoid hard insertion where the story stops and the character recites selling points.
- For 30 seconds, use one pain point, one turn, one CTA.
- For 60-90 seconds, allow one small arc with a proof point.
- For 180 seconds, allow character choice and emotional payoff, but do not expand background.

## CTA

CTA should match story temperature:

- Direct conversion: "Book", "Try", "Consult", "Download", "Join".
- Content continuation: "Watch next episode", "See the result", "Comment with your choice".
- Brand memory: short line, repeated prop, final visual, slogan only if earned.

CTA checks:

- Does it follow from the character's choice?
- Is the claim supportable?
- Is the disclosure handled if there is a paid or material connection?
- Is the platform mechanism needed, such as paid promotion or content disclosure, mentioned?

## Disclosure and Claims

- When there is sponsorship, payment, commission, gifts, brand partnership, affiliate interest, or product placement, flag the need for clear disclosure.
- Avoid unprovable efficacy claims, false scarcity, fake authority, fake user cases, guaranteed outcomes, or disguised paid endorsements.
- Raise risk for medical, financial, education, health, beauty, children/minor-related, and regulated products.
- If using music, footage, celebrity likeness, IP, or real brand references, flag clearance and licensing needs.

## Rewrite Rules

- Turn a sales point into conflict.
- Turn proof into visible evidence.
- Turn testimonial into a character-specific, non-universal experience.
- Turn slogan into a line that emerges from the scene.
- If a claim cannot be supported, replace it with a softer, experiential, or scene-based benefit.
