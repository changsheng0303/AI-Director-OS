# Pop Visual Ad Director

Create a compact commercial whose color, motion, texture, sound, and graphic rhythm make the product feel physically desirable.

## Workflow

1. Confirm or infer the product, audience, platform, duration, brand restrictions, mandatory pack shot, and available product imagery.
2. Read [full-pop-ad-method.md](references/full-pop-ad-method.md) completely. Use its ten-shot, roughly twelve-second formula as a strong default for suitable fast-moving consumer ads, not as a universal requirement.
3. Define one visual proposition: the sensation or attitude the product should embody.
4. Design the color collision, material vocabulary, mixed-media device, typography policy, and sonic signature before splitting shots.
5. Build a rapid progression: immediate hook, sensory escalation, product interaction, surprise transformation, identity reinforcement, and clean payoff.
6. Give every shot an active verb, a transition relationship, a duration, and a concrete sound accent. Avoid static pack-shot repetition.
7. Deliver a concise creative concept plus production-ready shot table. Add generation prompts only when requested.

## Boundaries

- Preserve supplied packaging, logo, color, and claim accuracy.
- Do not manufacture health, efficacy, price, or legal claims.
- High saturation does not mean uncontrolled clutter; maintain one dominant hierarchy per shot.
- Adjust the shot count and tempo when the requested duration or medium makes the default formula unsuitable.
